# Metric Calendar — mathematical research history

The research recommendation changed after the owner added **base-ten year structure as a mandatory design principle**.

**Current research design (owner decision, 21 October 2026):** [v3 — DC10 with month-local six-day weeks](v3-six-day-week-2026-10-21/README.md). Ten optimally balanced 36/37-day months; **six six-day weeks per month, four Focus Days then two Rest Days**; Day 37 in any long month is a **Bonus Rest Day** outside that month's regular weeks. In leap years the central Month 05 Day 37 is the sixth Bonus Rest Day (also called Leap Rest Day), counted once. Annual totals are 240 Focus Days and 125/126 designated Rest Days. The selected six-day-week rule is a deliberate design preference, not a mathematical proof of ideal human recovery.

**Prior v2 (historical mathematical foundation):** [base-ten DC10 derivation](v2-base10-2026-10-20/README.md) proved the optimally balanced ten-month lengths and central leap placement but proposed that weeks be optional; that **weekly-layer decision is superseded by v3**. The v2 decimal arithmetic and dated tests remain relevant.

**Prior research (historical):** [v1 — ordinal solar calendar](v1-2026-10-20/README.md) was optimal for **minimum compulsory subdivisions**, but by design did not require a genuine base-ten hierarchy. Its standalone conclusion is **superseded under the amended design constraint**; the astronomical, conversion and human-rhythm analysis remains relevant source material.

**Production status:** Neither v1, v2 nor v3 replaces `calendar.js`, the website converter, Gregorian January 1 alignment or the existing six-day/week presentation. The public site continues to implement the original ten-month ordering and terminal leap date. The latest **research** model has different month ordering, a central leap-rest date and provisional astronomical epoch; it must not silently reinterpret existing stored Metric dates.

Develop and test owner-locally on GMKtec by default when accessible. Public GitHub standard CI and Pages remain free, independent validation; do not attach the owner's personal PC to untrusted public PR code.
